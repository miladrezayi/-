# Siren Radio sound library

The APK build downloads these source recordings into Android `res/raw` before compilation. The app plays the bundled local files, so sound playback itself does not need an internet connection.

## Emergency sirens

| App asset | Source clip | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `police_siren_fr_outdoor.mp3` | [French police siren, outdoor](https://sfxjar.com/police-siren/) | Real outdoor recording of a French Gendarmerie two-tone siren |
| `police_siren_fr_passing.mp3` | [French police siren, passing](https://sfxjar.com/police-siren/) | Real recording of the siren passing left to right |
| `ambulance_siren_outdoor.mp3` | [Ambulance, 3 Ton Siren](https://sfxjar.com/ambulance/) | Real outdoor recording, without engine noise |
| `ambulance_siren_engine.mp3` | [Ambulance, 3 ton siren, motor](https://sfxjar.com/ambulance/) | Real recording with the engine running |
| `gendarmerie_motorcycle_siren.mp3` | [Gendarmerie motorcycle crossing](https://sfxjar.com/siren/) | Field recording of motorcycles passing with a siren |
| `national_alert_siren.mp3` | [National Alert Signal, Rémalard (61)](https://sfxjar.com/siren/) | Civil warning siren recorded in France in October 2024 |

## Walkie-talkie / radio

| App asset | Source clip | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `walkie_start.mp3` | [Walkie talkie: Start](https://sfxjar.com/walkie-talkie/) | Real Midland G7 radio start-up recording |
| `walkie_keypad.mp3` | [Walkie talkie: Keypad tones](https://sfxjar.com/walkie-talkie/) | Real keypad tones recorded from a Midland G7 |
| `walkie_conversation.mp3` | [Walkie talkie: Conversation](https://sfxjar.com/walkie-talkie/) | Recorded radio conversation/background from a Midland G7 |
| `radio_interference.mp3` | [Radio Interference #1](https://sfxjar.com/static/) | Recorded interference on two radio channels |

The source pages mark the listed BigSoundBank recordings as **CC0 1.0**. SFX Jar explains that CC0 clips may be reused in apps, including commercial apps, without attribution: [licence explanation](https://sfxjar.com/free-to-use/).

The app does not impersonate or transmit on emergency-service radio frequencies. These clips are playback sound effects only.
